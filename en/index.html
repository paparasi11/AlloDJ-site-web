Face B · B4JoinCo-optation

Entry is by recommendation, not application

Joining the AlloDJ roster runs through co-optation: it takes at least 3 DJs already active to vouch for the new profile. There is no open application form — the reputation of those who recommend is tied to that of the DJ they recommend, and the other way round.

Face B · B5The questionsFAQ

What we get asked most

How do I book a DJ in Douala?

Enter the date, the venue and your budget in the AlloDJ app. The platform picks a DJ available in Douala on that date, suited to your type of event. The booking is confirmed with a 10% deposit.

How do I find a DJ in Yaoundé?

It works exactly as in Douala: booking happens in the app, and the DJ offered is chosen among those covering Yaoundé. No bidding war, no quotes to compare.

How much does booking a DJ in Cameroon cost?

You state the budget: AlloDJ looks for a DJ within that range rather than making you compare quotes. The only fixed rule is the 10% deposit at booking, with the balance settled 3 days before the event. The price depends on the DJ, the duration and the equipment requested.

Can I listen to AlloDJ DJs' music before booking?

Yes. The platform's mixtapes and sets are free to listen to — the best preview of what you're booking. Full listening happens in the app.

How does AlloDJ pick the DJ?

The platform crosses three criteria: the date (the DJ must be free), the lieu (Douala or Yaoundé today) and the budget you state. It then picks one specific profile — your request isn't broadcast to a list of DJs who would all call you back.

How much do I pay when booking?

A deposit of 10% of the agreed amount confirms the booking. The balance is settled 3 days before the event. No other fee is charged at booking time.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation is free within 48 hours of booking. After that, the deposit stays with the DJ who blocked their date.

How do you become a DJ on AlloDJ?

You don't apply. Entry happens on the recommendation of at least 3 DJs already active on the platform. They vouch for the level and the reliability of the new profile.

Which cities does AlloDJ cover?

Douala et Yaoundé, in Cameroon. More cities will follow as the roster grows.

For which types of event?

Weddings, traditional ceremonies and dowry, company parties, birthdays, christenings, graduations, homecomings, concerts and clubs. The type of event changes the running order and the repertoire — the recommendation takes that into account.

Can I rent the sound system too?

Yes. Sound-system rental is booked in the app, in the same place as the DJ.

Is there a mobile app?

AlloDJ works in the browser today, at appli.allodeejay.com. App Store and Google Play versions are coming.
